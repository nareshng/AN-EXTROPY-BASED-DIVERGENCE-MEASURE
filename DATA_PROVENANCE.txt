4. MRI image data
-----------------
Stated source:
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/sartajbhuvaji/brain-tumor-classification-mri/data

Files analysed: NT1.jpg-NT3.jpg, BT1.jpg-BT3.jpg and MT1.jpg-MT3.jpg.
Required record: Real Data Analysis/Images/image_manifest.csv.

For every image, the completed manifest must contain:
- original filename or source record identifier;
- native source class exactly as supplied by the dataset;
- documented mapping to NT, BT or MT;
- selection rule and who performed the selection;
- source URL and retrieval date;
- intellectual-property holder and license;
- whether redistribution in the journal supplement is permitted;
- MD5 checksum and original dimensions.

The current renamed image files alone are insufficient to verify their source
class or the benign/malignant relabelling. Do not make diagnostic or biological
claims until this mapping has been documented and independently checked.
